Your forwarding address

{{ inbound_address }}

Anything sent to this address is read for job postings and added to your feed. We never see your inbox, only the emails you forward.

Gmail: forward your job alerts
  1. In Gmail open Settings (the gear), then See all settings, then Forwarding and POP/IMAP.
  2. Press Add a forwarding address and paste the address above. Gmail sends it a confirmation. A banner with a link appears here and on your dashboard within a few minutes. Click it to confirm.
  3. Go to Filters and Blocked Addresses, then Create a new filter. In From put the sender of your alerts, for example jobalerts-noreply@linkedin.com.
  4. Press Create filter, tick Forward it to, choose your forwarding address, and save.
Outlook: forward your job alerts
  1. Open Settings, then Mail, then Rules, and press Add new rule.
  2. Condition: Sender address includes the alert sender, for example linkedin.com.
  3. Action: Forward to and paste the address above. Save.

LinkedIn alerts work best. Other job sites go through a general reader that is less precise. We only read the email you send us and never open LinkedIn for you.
